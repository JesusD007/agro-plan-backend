## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid — structured extraction and bounded content generation.

**Description:**
The .NET backend supports three producer-facing AI workflows: transcribe Dominican Spanish or Haitian Creole voice notes into editable, incomplete activity drafts; draft weekly farm summaries from confirmed records and pending tasks; and describe visible symptoms from private farm photos for agronomist review. Good output is structured, source-grounded, explicit about missing or uncertain information, private to the authorized farm, and never changes authoritative records without user or agronomist confirmation.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. A transcription or summary must never write or alter a confirmed logbook record; activity drafts require explicit user confirmation.
2. Numeric totals and source references in summaries must come from deterministic, farm-scoped structured data; generated prose cannot invent quantities or facts.
3. Image analysis must describe visible signs only; diagnosis and treatment are not approved until a verified agronomist authorized for that farm confirms them.
4. Uncertain transcription, missing data, unavailable models, or insufficient images must be surfaced as partial, pending, or unavailable states rather than fabricated.
5. Provider payloads and persisted results must not disclose data across farms or exceed the user's and advisor's current authorization scopes.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Smallholder farm operations and agricultural extension in the Dominican Republic.

**User Population:** Producers and farm administrators record field work and review weekly activity; assigned farm workers dictate task records; authorized agronomists review crop-symptom reports. Dominican Spanish and Haitian Creole speakers are in scope.

**Stakes Level:** High. A wrong activity draft can corrupt a farm record if not corrected, while a misleading crop-health assessment can prompt delayed escalation or harmful field action. Human confirmation and agronomist review are mandatory authority boundaries.

**Output Consequence:** Voice output remains an editable draft until the user confirms it. Weekly narrative may inform farm decisions and can be shared only under an active, scope-compatible advisor grant. Symptom descriptions remain pending until an explicitly authorized agronomist has enough evidence and confirms a diagnosis or treatment.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

| Dimension | Good (domain expert accepts) | Bad (expert flags) | Stakes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Field-note fidelity across languages | The editable draft preserves the speaker's stated activity, date, parcel/crop, quantity and unit; uncertain speech and missing fields remain visibly uncertain. Numbers, units, negation, crop names, and Spanish–Kreyòl code-switching are checked with bilingual field recordings and human reference transcripts. | A plausible but unstated date, quantity, unit, task, or crop is inserted; Kreyòl terms are silently translated into a different action; uncertainty is hidden. | High | REQ-rf-g-39; Haitian Creole ASR research notes limited resources and separately evaluates transcription quality ([Havard et al., Interspeech 2025](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/havard25_interspeech.pdf), [Radio Haiti-Inter, LREC 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.241/)). |
| Weekly farm-account fidelity | Every stated activity and pending task belongs to the selected farm/week (and cycle filter); numeric totals equal deterministic projections in their original units and can be traced to permitted source records; missing records are disclosed. | Draft prose invents, misattributes, or duplicates a task, blends unlike units, includes unconfirmed records, or discloses a source outside the viewer's grant. | High | REQ-rf-g-40; Phase 2's source-backed unit and report rules in `.planning/phases/02-farm-operations-and-safety/02-CONTEXT.md`. |
| Crop-symptom observation and escalation | The report separates visible signs (affected plant part, appearance, extent) from a cause, identifies missing/poor-quality imagery, requests useful additional photos or context, and stays pending for agronomist review. | It presents an image-based cause as certain, mistakes abiotic stress or damage for a disease, names a treatment before review, or accepts imagery too poor to assess. | Critical | REQ-rf-g-43; plant-health field guidance treats diagnosis and management recommendations as work for trained plant-health advisors using available evidence ([CABI Plantwise Diagnostic Field Guide](https://www.cabi.org/cabi-publications/plantwise-diagnostic-field-guide/), [FAO rural advisory methods guide](https://www.fao.org/uploads/media/A%20decision%20guide.pdf)). |
| Human review and report authority | Only the verified agronomist authorized for that farm confirms diagnosis/treatment; requests for additional evidence leave the case pending and the review action is attributable. | A producer, worker, general advisor, or model output is treated as the agronomist's approval; an inconclusive case is silently closed or treated as safe. | Critical | REQ-rf-g-43 and Phase 4 decisions D-08–D-10 in `04-CONTEXT.md`; plant-clinic practice uses trained plant doctors/advisors for diagnostic advice (FAO guide above). |

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Field audio distorts operational facts:** wind, machinery, distance from the phone, unfamiliar crop names, dialect, code-switching, and number/unit pronunciation can change a spoken amount or task while leaving a fluent transcript. Haitian Creole resources are more limited than common-language ASR resources; accuracy must be measured on consented local field recordings, not inferred from generic multilingual claims.
2. **Similar-looking crop damage is overinterpreted:** pests, disease, nutrient issues, water stress, chemical injury, and physical damage may produce overlapping visible signs. A single close-up may omit the crop, affected plant parts, field distribution, growth stage, or environmental context an agronomist needs; ask for evidence and abstain.
3. **Summary language outruns its evidence:** a missing logbook entry can be mistaken for no activity, a pending task can be described as completed, or numbers with unlike units/currencies can be combined into a plausible but invalid total. Keep the structured projection authoritative and expose missing data.
4. **Review scope is confused:** advisor access or farm membership may change after a summary/report was created. Recheck current farm membership and grant scope when viewing or sharing; do not assume an earlier approval grants continuing access.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

Dominican Republic Law 172-13 is a relevant personal-data and privacy constraint for recordings, worker-related audio, report photos, and media that may include people, location, or identifiable farm details. Apply purpose limitation, access control, agreed retention/deletion, and secure processor handling; obtain a deployment-specific legal review before production data is sent to an external model provider. The law is described as current by the [Superintendencia de Bancos](https://www.sb.gob.do/regulacion/leyes/ley-no-172-13-proteccion-de-los-datos/) and is listed in the [Presidency legal framework](https://www.presidencia.gob.do/transparencia/marco-legal/leyes). No separate AI-specific Dominican rule was established by this research. Existing Phase 2 rules still require exact validated evidence for any pesticide/harvest safety determination; Phase 4 AI must not supply or override those values.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** No additional AI/agent framework. Use existing provider-neutral application contracts and invoke the provider SDK inside .NET Worker adapters.

**Version:** N/A — no framework added. Existing Gemini adapter uses `Google.GenAI` 1.28.0; model selection remains configurable.

**Rationale:**
These are bounded transcription/extraction and content-drafting operations with human approval, not model-controlled workflows. Existing .NET Worker consumers, RabbitMQ/outbox, PostgreSQL state, and SignalR job notifications already provide orchestration. Keeping provider request/response types behind neutral contracts supports the user's provider-swapping requirement without introducing a second runtime or orchestration dependency.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| Microsoft Agent Framework | No multi-agent or model-controlled tool workflow is required; adding orchestration creates unnecessary complexity. Reconsider only if a later phase needs stateful agent workflows. |
| LangChain / LangGraph | Existing .NET Worker and outbox are sufficient; a Python/second runtime would add operational surface without a scoped need. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — Google Gemini remains the current provider for existing proposal workflows. Phase 4 speech/image/summary ports stay provider-neutral; validate and select each provider before production activation, with Haitian Creole support as a hard speech gate.

### Entry Point Pattern
```csharp
// Keep this inside an infrastructure adapter; do not expose Client or SDK types
// through AgroPlan.Application contracts.
var client = new Client(apiKey: configuration["GEMINI_API_KEY"]);
var response = await client.Models.GenerateContentAsync(
    model: configuredModel,
    contents: boundedInput,
    config: new GenerateContentConfig
    {
        Temperature = 0.1,
        MaxOutputTokens = configuredOutputLimit,
        ResponseMimeType = "application/json",
        ResponseJsonSchema = JsonNode.Parse(outputJsonSchema)
    });

var json = response.Candidates?.FirstOrDefault()?.Content?.Parts?
    .FirstOrDefault()?.Text
    ?? throw new InvalidOperationException("Provider returned no structured content.");
var parsed = JsonSerializer.Deserialize<ActivityDraftOutput>(json, jsonOptions)
    ?? throw new JsonException("Provider returned an empty activity draft.");
ValidateSemantics(parsed); // JSON-schema conformance is not domain validation.
```

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `Client` / `Models.GenerateContentAsync` | Provider SDK client and non-streaming content generation call | Bounded summary prose, structured text extraction, or image description inside a Gemini adapter. |
| `GenerateContentConfig` | Per-call system instruction, temperature/token limits, modality, safety and output configuration | Set explicit output budgets and request JSON schemas; never rely on model defaults. |
| `ResponseJsonSchema` | JSON Schema-constrained response configuration | Extract activity fields and create constrained report descriptions; validate semantics and authorization in .NET afterwards. |
| `Part` / `Content` and Files API | Multimodal input parts and temporary uploaded file handles | Send a validated private audio/image payload only from the Worker; delete provider-side files when no longer required. |
| `IAiProposalProvider` | Existing provider-neutral application boundary, currently implemented by `GeminiProposalProvider` | Keep Gemini SDK calls, prompts, errors, model names, and output parsing outside domain/application code. Add narrow speech/summary/vision contracts if combining operations would make the current contract unclear. |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Assuming Haitian Creole is supported because a model is multilingual.** Google documents Gemini 3.5 Transcribe support for 85+ locales, but this research did not confirm Haitian Creole in its published locale list. Keep Creole transcription disabled until representative Haitian Creole field audio passes an evaluated provider test; use a replaceable speech adapter if Gemini does not meet the requirement.
2. **Treating constrained JSON as valid business data.** Gemini supports only a subset of JSON Schema and its documentation still requires application-level validation. Reject invalid enum values, dates, units, farm/parcel references, and unsupported claims; leave uncertain fields missing.
3. **Using `ResponseSchema` without stable property ordering.** The .NET SDK warns that a dictionary does not guarantee schema property order. Prefer `ResponseJsonSchema` built from `JsonNode`, or set `PropertyOrdering` explicitly.
4. **Using free-tier AI Studio/API for real farm audio or photos.** Google says content submitted to unpaid services can be used to improve products and may be reviewed by people. Production must use an approved paid service/account configuration and the project privacy review; remove unnecessary identifiers and never send another farm's data.
5. **Assuming provider file deletion follows AgroPlan retention automatically.** Gemini File API uploads are temporary (documented for 48 hours); this is separate from AgroPlan's 7-day audio policy and report-photo deletion rules. Track provider file IDs and issue explicit deletion after processing, with cleanup retry and no raw media in logs.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
Treat model IDs as deployment configuration, separate per task, and never persist vendor model names in domain records. The official Gemini 3.5 Transcribe model is purpose-built for speech-to-text, supports files up to one hour, and is sufficient for the product's five-minute recording cap; the price page currently estimates about $0.005 per audio minute on paid Standard (approximately $0.025 for five minutes, excluding the separate extraction call). This is a planning estimate, not a budget guarantee. Use a separate configured multimodal model for image descriptions and bounded Spanish summary prose. Start with low temperature and explicit `MaxOutputTokens`; benchmark exact settings against the reference set before release. Do not enable Haitian Creole until locale support and accuracy are verified. The output model may vary by operation; no single model is confirmed here for all three tasks.

**Core Pattern:**
Use a persisted, idempotent background job. The API verifies the current farm membership, role/task assignment, media type/size, consent, and authorization before staging private input; it commits job state and an outbox event atomically. The Worker loads only the authorized records or media, sends the minimum required payload to the provider-neutral adapter, validates the response against a versioned schema and deterministic domain rules, and stores a draft/report result with missing/uncertain fields. The user explicitly confirms a voice draft before append-only logbook creation. A report remains pending until an authorized agronomist confirms diagnosis/treatment. Provider failures remain retryable or explicitly unavailable; they never create authoritative records.

**Tool Use:**
These workflows need no model function-calling or model-controlled tools. When Gemini is the approved provider for an operation, use its server-side API key only in the Worker adapter. Keep uploads in the private Supabase bucket and send provider files directly from the Worker after authorization; do not expose provider file URIs or credentials to clients. Use explicit safety settings where appropriate, but safety filters do not replace the application's agronomic abstention rules. For production data, use the paid Gemini service configuration: unpaid service content may be used to improve Google products and may be subject to human review; paid-service prompts/responses are not used for product improvement, though limited abuse-monitoring retention still applies. Confirm the account/project billing and terms posture with the project owner before enabling real farm media.

**State Management:**
PostgreSQL is authoritative for job status, draft/report content, source record IDs, schema/prompt versions, agronomist review and confirmation. Store provider request IDs and uploaded-file IDs only when needed for idempotency and explicit cleanup. Make retries idempotent by job ID; do not duplicate a draft or logbook entry on redelivery. Delete original audio when the user confirms or no later than seven days after processing; delete symptom photos when the review closes or is rejected while preserving the report. Add a cleanup retry state for provider and object-storage deletion failures, and never log transcripts, image bytes, access tokens, or sensitive model payloads.

**Context Window Strategy:**
Keep each call single-purpose and farm-scoped. Transcription receives only audio plus the language hints supported by the chosen provider; extraction receives the transcript and the minimum allowed parcel/cycle lookup context; weekly summaries receive only confirmed source records, pending tasks, missing-data markers, and deterministic totals/source IDs for the selected farm week; image analysis receives only the authorized report photos and the selected crop/parcel/stage context needed to describe visible signs. Exclude names, contact details, exact location, credentials, unrelated farm records, and unsupported crop rules. Do not reuse cached prompts or media across tenants. Truncate or split inputs using explicit limits and return a partial/unavailable status rather than silently omitting source records.

**API Evolution Gate:**
Google's current Gemini docs promote the newer Interactions API and describe `generateContent` as legacy-but-supported. The current pinned `Google.GenAI` 1.28.0 examples and established code use `GenerateContentAsync`; verify whether the pinned .NET SDK has the needed Interactions API and audio/image features before adopting it. If not, either retain the supported API behind the provider adapter with an explicit migration note or use the REST endpoint only inside that adapter. Do not let this uncertainty leak into application contracts.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs and C# Validation

Gemini's constrained JSON support accepts a subset of JSON Schema; the .NET SDK supports `GenerateContentConfig.ResponseJsonSchema`. Keep the C# DTO as the application contract and define the provider schema in the adapter. Required properties may contain `null` when the model cannot identify a value; the application computes the `missingFields` and `uncertainFields` lists and rejects unknown activity kinds or fabricated dates/units. Parse with `System.Text.Json`, then run ordinary .NET validation and same-farm/assignment checks before persistence.

```csharp
public sealed record ActivityDraftOutput(
    string? ActivityType,
    DateOnly? OccurredOn,
    string? ParcelReference,
    string? CropCode,
    decimal? Quantity,
    string? Unit,
    IReadOnlyList<string> UncertainFields);

// Example schema fragment. Include the remaining properties and mark nullable
// fields as required-but-nullable if that schema feature is supported by the
// configured model; always validate the parsed C# object afterwards.
var schema = JsonNode.Parse("""
{
  "type": "object",
  "properties": {
    "activityType": {"type": ["string", "null"]},
    "occurredOn": {"type": ["string", "null"], "format": "date"},
    "parcelReference": {"type": ["string", "null"]},
    "cropCode": {"type": ["string", "null"]},
    "quantity": {"type": ["number", "null"]},
    "unit": {"type": ["string", "null"]},
    "uncertainFields": {"type": "array", "items": {"type": "string"}}
  },
  "required": ["activityType", "occurredOn", "parcelReference", "cropCode", "quantity", "unit", "uncertainFields"]
}
""");
```

Do not copy the full JSON schema or JSON example into the prompt; Google warns this can reduce structured-output quality. Verify nullable unions and date formats against the selected model's supported schema subset. On malformed JSON or semantic validation failure, make at most one bounded repair/regeneration attempt using the same allowed source data; log request/job ID, schema version, provider/model, latency, token usage and failure category, but not raw media or transcript. If the second response fails, persist a failed/needs-user-correction state without creating a record.

### Async-First Design

The .NET SDK exposes asynchronous model calls; await them from Worker consumers and pass cancellation tokens through SDK, storage, and database calls. Do not block with `.Result`/`.Wait()` inside ASP.NET Core or the Worker, which can exhaust threads and ignore cancellation. Use complete async responses for schema-constrained drafts and summaries so the full response can be validated before persistence. Streaming is unnecessary for these bounded jobs; if later used for progress, stream only non-authoritative status and never persist or display partial output as a reviewed result.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

Separate stable system instructions (role, abstention rules, output language and bounds) from serialized, validated per-job source data. Treat transcripts, farm notes, crop names, image text, and user-controlled values as untrusted data, never instructions. Do not give the model database IDs, mutation tools, or permission to call APIs. For summaries, state that only listed confirmed facts and pending-task facts may be mentioned; calculate all numbers before the model call and validate cited source IDs afterwards. For images, require observable visual descriptions and an explicit `insufficientImage`/`additionalPhotosNeeded` response; no diagnosis or treatment field is available before agronomist review. Use a small reviewed evaluation set rather than dynamic retrieval/few-shot examples at launch. Set output-token limits and bound prompt/input size on every call.

### Context Window Management

These are stateless jobs; do not carry conversational history across farm requests. Partition a long summary into bounded, deterministic farm/cycle/week projections before drafting, retaining source IDs and unit/currency groupings so no totals are reconstructed by the model. If the source projection exceeds configured limits, mark the summary incomplete and list omitted/missing sources rather than silently summarizing a subset. Compress images only in a documented private-storage processing step that preserves symptom-relevant detail and original evidence; never substitute a lossy thumbnail without confirming adequacy. Uploaded Gemini Files persist temporarily (Google documents 48 hours), so use inline parts for small inputs where supported or explicitly delete uploaded files after processing and retry deletion failures.

### Cost and Latency Budget

Current Gemini Developer API pricing lists Gemini 3.5 Transcribe at an estimated **$0.005 per audio minute** on paid Standard, or about **$0.025 for a five-minute recording** using the page's blended estimate. This excludes the structured extraction call and retries. Text-summary/image model cost depends on the selected model, image resolution and output length; instrument those dimensions and establish a per-job cap before production. Do not cache farm audio, images, transcripts, or cross-tenant prompts. Reuse only static schemas/system prompts in process memory; cache deterministic report projections only if scoped by farm, time window, and data version. Route each task to its lowest-cost model that meets its own evaluation threshold, and cap retries at one schema repair plus bounded transient network retries. Free-tier inputs may be used for product improvement; production farm content requires the approved paid-service posture.

### Unresolved Provider Gate — Haitian Creole

The user selected Dominican Spanish and Haitian Creole. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is documented for 85+ locales, but the official language listing available during this research did not let me verify Haitian Creole support. This is an explicit pre-production gate: test representative, consented Haitian Creole and code-switched Dominican field recordings with the selected provider and a human-verified reference transcript. If supported locale coverage or accuracy is insufficient, keep the neutral speech-provider contract and select/approve a compatible provider before enabling Creole; do not silently fall back to Spanish-only transcription.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric (Pass/Fail or 1-5) | Measurement Approach | Priority |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------------------|----------|
| Voice field fidelity and uncertainty | **PASS:** For each clearly audible activity/date/parcel/crop/quantity/unit, the draft preserves the spoken value; missing or unclear values remain null and are marked uncertain. Field-level exact accuracy is at least 95% per supported language on the labeled reference corpus. **FAIL:** A clear number, unit, negation, activity or crop is changed, or an uncertain value is silently filled. | Code-based field comparison plus bilingual speaker review; report per-language and code-switch metrics separately. | Critical |
| Draft authority and completion | **PASS:** Every successful voice job yields an editable draft and creates zero logbook entries until an authorized user explicitly confirms; duplicate retries create no duplicate draft/entry. **FAIL:** Model output writes a record, replay duplicates it, or confirmation bypasses authorization. | xUnit v3 unit and PostgreSQL/RabbitMQ integration tests; assert exactly zero writes before confirmation and one idempotent write after. | Critical |
| Summary source faithfulness and numeric integrity | **PASS:** Every numeric total exactly matches the deterministic projection in its original unit/currency; every stated task/fact belongs to the selected farm/week/cycle; missing data is disclosed; source references are complete and authorized. **FAIL:** Any invented/duplicated total, unconfirmed record, incompatible-unit aggregation, wrong week, or unauthorized source appears. | 100% code validation of values/source IDs and farm scope; farm manager review of narrative for usefulness and clarity. | Critical |
| Advisor scope | **PASS:** A shared summary is available only after owner/administrator approval and only while an active grant includes the summary/source scope. **FAIL:** Revoked/expired grants or out-of-scope sources remain readable. | PostgreSQL/API integration tests for role, grant scope, expiry, revocation, and source access. | Critical |
| Symptom observation and abstention | **PASS:** The pre-review report names only visible signs, identifies insufficient imagery and requests useful additional evidence; no diagnosis/treatment is produced or marked approved. At least 90% of expert-labeled insufficient-image cases request more evidence. **FAIL:** Disease/cause certainty, treatment, or a safety instruction is presented before authorized agronomist review. | Schema/domain-code assertions for authority fields; agronomist review of visual fidelity and evidence requests. | Critical |
| Agronomist review authority | **PASS:** Only a verified agronomist assigned/authorized to the farm can confirm diagnosis/treatment; requests for more evidence remain pending and every decision is attributable. **FAIL:** Producer, worker, generic advisor, or model can approve; rejected/closed reports still accept changes. | xUnit v3 and PostgreSQL/API integration tests across authorized and unauthorized identities and lifecycle transitions. | Critical |
| Domain narrative quality | **PASS:** At least 90% of reviewed summary and symptom narratives score 4/5 or higher for clear Dominican Spanish, evidence fidelity, and helpfulness, with no critical safety violation. **FAIL:** Narrative obscures uncertainty, confuses pending/verified work, or reads an observation as approved agronomic advice. | Human evaluation by farm managers and Dominican agronomists; do not rely on an uncalibrated LLM judge. | High |

### Reference Dataset

**Size:** Start with at least 45 labeled cases: 25 voice (10 Dominican Spanish, 10 Haitian Creole, 5 Spanish–Kreyòl code-switch), 10 weekly summaries, and 10 symptom-photo reports. Before enabling a speech provider for production, expand to at least 20 consented, representative recordings in each supported language plus code-switch cases and meet the field-accuracy gate separately for each language.

**Composition:**
Voice cases cover clear and noisy field audio, activity names, dates, crop/parcel references, quantities and units, negation, missing fields, uncertain spans, and code-switching. Summary cases cover empty weeks, pending versus verified tasks, corrections, missing records, farm time-zone boundaries, crop-cycle filters, unlike units/currencies, and authorized/revoked advisor sources. Image cases cover adequate and inadequate focus/lighting, close-up versus context photos, visually ambiguous damage, missing plant-part views, and cases where more photos are required. Use fictional farm identifiers and de-identified or synthetically generated metadata.

**Labeling:**
Haitian Creole speakers with agricultural vocabulary and Dominican Spanish speakers produce/check reference transcripts and uncertainty spans. Farm owners/administrators label task status, source fidelity and summary usefulness. Dominican agronomists label only visible signs, adequacy of images and whether more evidence is needed; they define any diagnosis/treatment gold labels only after review. A privacy reviewer checks consent and de-identification. Store a versioned reference manifest and rubric; keep source media private and delete it according to the approved evaluation retention policy.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Farm/role authorization | No active farm membership, worker task assignment, agronomist assignment, or current advisor grant for requested data | Reject before media retrieval/provider call; re-check scope when results are read or shared. |
| Schema and deterministic domain validation | Malformed response, unknown activity/unit/source, unsupported number/date, out-of-scope source, or incomplete required job metadata | Reject provider result; allow at most one bounded repair attempt where safe, then persist a correctable partial/failed state without creating an authoritative record. |
| Voice confirmation boundary | Any path attempts to append a logbook entry before explicit authorized confirmation | Block append; only the authenticated confirmation endpoint may create one idempotent immutable entry. |
| Summary grounding | Any total differs from the server-calculated projection or cites a source outside selected farm/week/cycle or active grant | Discard narrative and return a safe unavailable/error state; never show an ungrounded total. |
| Symptom abstention and reviewer boundary | Pre-review output includes diagnosis/treatment fields, insufficient-image case lacks a follow-up request, or reviewer is not a verified agronomist authorized for the farm | Do not persist/show as an approved assessment; keep pending and request more evidence or authorized review. |
| Media consent and retention | Missing consent, expired audio retention deadline, closed/rejected symptom report, or provider file not deleted | Block external processing without consent; delete media and retry cleanup; alert on cleanup backlog. |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Voice exact-field accuracy, uncertainty recall, and word error by language | Review all low-confidence, user-corrected, repeated, and failed jobs; randomly review a small consented sample per language. | Pause the affected language/provider if critical-field accuracy is below 95% or an unsupported language is detected; relabel and evaluate a replacement before re-enable. |
| Summary factuality, task-status accuracy, and traceability | Sample summaries with edits, missing data, unusual totals, grant changes, and a random baseline; compare every number/source against the deterministic projection. | Block release on any unsupported numeric or cross-farm claim; adjust prompts/projection and add regression cases. |
| Symptom visual-grounding and abstention quality | Agronomists review all reports needing additional photos, rejected/returned reports, and a random selection of completed reports. | Keep diagnosis/treatment disabled until the agronomist gate passes; add newly observed ambiguity patterns to the image reference set. |
| Audio/photo deletion completion and access-policy events | Daily aggregate review of cleanup retries and denied authorization events; no media content sampled. | Resolve storage/provider deletion failures, expire orphaned objects, and investigate repeated authorization anomalies. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Arize Phoenix, self-hosted in a private environment and reached via OTLP/OpenTelemetry. It is the trace viewer, not the source of truth. If tracing is unavailable, application job state and structured aggregate metrics continue without logging AI payloads. Deploy only after privacy review and an approved data-processing configuration.

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Per-operation job completion, retry, failure and p50/p95 latency (voice, summary, symptom report).
- Voice partial/uncertain-field rate and human correction rate by enabled language/provider.
- Summary grounding rejections, source-validation failures, and explicit user edits.
- Symptom reports requesting additional evidence, agronomist return/rejection rate, and pre-review abstention violations (target zero).
- Provider cost/tokens per job and pending/failed audio or photo cleanup count/age.

**Alert Thresholds:**
- Page/stop the affected operation immediately on any authorization leak, unconfirmed logbook write, pre-review diagnosis/treatment approval, or cross-farm source disclosure (zero tolerance).
- Pause the affected speech language if rolling labeled field accuracy falls below 95% or its provider has not passed the Creole compatibility/quality gate.
- Alert when a job's retry/failure rate exceeds 5% over 15 minutes, or p95 latency exceeds the agreed release SLO for 30 minutes; establish the numeric latency SLO from pre-release measurements before launch.
- Alert on any audio past its seven-day deadline or closed/rejected symptom image not deleted after the configured cleanup retry window; surface cleanup failures immediately to operations.
- Alert on daily spend exceeding the configured per-operation budget; budgets must be set from measured pilot volume before production enablement.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**
Prioritize human review for low-confidence/partial voice drafts, corrected quantities/dates/units, new language or code-switch patterns, summaries edited by managers, missing-data weeks, unusual totals, advisor grant changes, reports requesting more photos, agronomist returns, and provider/schema errors. Add a small random sample across all enabled operations to detect silent failures. Reviewers see authorized, consented examples through a private workflow; telemetry spans contain only opaque job correlation and aggregate technical metadata, never raw media, transcript, prompt, summary, farm/user IDs, or credentials. Delete evaluation copies according to the explicit retention policy and record reviewer decisions as evaluation labels, not farm facts.
